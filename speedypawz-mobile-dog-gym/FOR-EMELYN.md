# Your new booking page: how it works

Hi Emelyn! Here's everything you need to know about the page in one place.

## Where requests show up
Every time someone sends a request, you get an **email at info@speedypawz.com**. The subject line tells you who it is at a glance:

- **New Speedy PawZ request: Kira (Woodland)** means someone wants a session. Kira is the dog's name, Woodland is their city.
- **New Speedy PawZ call request: Kiko (Evening)** means someone wants a quick call first. It shows the dog's name (or the person's name) and the best time to call.

## The one rule: reply fast
**Text them within a few minutes if you can.** The first business to reply usually gets the booking. People who fill out a form are ready right now, and that feeling fades fast.

A simple first text:
> Hi [name]! It's Emelyn from Speedy PawZ. Thanks for your request for [dog's name]! Does [their first time choice] still work for you?

If they checked the texting box, they said yes to texts. If they didn't, call or email instead.

## What each field means
| Field | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Dog's name, breed, size, age | The basics about the pup |
| My dog is... | High energy, slowing down, shy around other dogs, or not sure |
| Services | What they're interested in (first session, weekly, two dogs, meet-and-greet, not sure) |
| Choice 1 and Choice 2 | Two days and times of day that work for them. Choice 2 may be blank |
| First name, last name, mobile, email | How to reach them |
| City and street | Where they are, and where to park |
| Best way to reach you | Text, call or email. Use the one they picked |
| Anything else | Their notes, like treats, quirks or health info |
| Texting box | "Yes" means they agreed to texts about their request |
| Source | Which ad or link brought them (for example "instagram / blackfriday") |

## Want to change something?
**Text Zach** to change prices, hours, services, the Black Friday special, FAQ answers or anything else on the page. Most changes take a few minutes.

## Things we still need from you
- Your prices (first session, weekly regulars) and the Black Friday details and end date
- The days and hours you work, and how far you'll drive
- Is the meet-and-greet free? What happens if a dog isn't ready to step on?
- Size, age and vaccine requirements, and whether owners need to be home
- How fast you can usually reply (so we can promise it on the page)
- OK to show the photo with Adan? Which husky is Kira and which is Kiko?
- Permission from the owners of the client dogs in the photos
- A few real Google reviews you're happy to quote, with first names
- Your booking calendar link, if you use one (Square, Calendly or similar)
- An intro video, if you'd like one on the page
