Privacy policy

Speedy PawZ Mobile Dog Gym, Woodland, California. Last updated November 19, 2026.

The short version

We only collect what you type into our request forms. We use it to reply to you and schedule your pup's session. We never sell it.

What we collect

When you send a session request or a call request, we collect what you enter, which can include:

  • Your first and last name, mobile number and email
  • Your city and, if you choose, a street or cross streets so we know where to park
  • Your dog's name, breed, size, age and anything you tell us about them
  • The services and times you're interested in, and your notes or questions
  • Whether you agreed to receive text messages from us

We also record which ad or link brought you to the page (for example, an Instagram or Facebook ad), so we know what's working. We don't collect payment information on this website.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and schedule a session or a call
  • To send appointment details, reminders and photo updates you asked for
  • To keep notes about your dog so every session fits them

Text messages

If you check the box on our form, Speedy PawZ may text you about your request and your sessions. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Agreeing to texts is never a condition of booking. We don't share your mobile number or your text opt-in with anyone for their marketing.

Who processes your information

Our website is hosted by Netlify, Inc., and our forms are processed by Netlify Forms. When you submit a form, Netlify stores your answers and sends them to us by email. Netlify handles this information on our behalf under its own privacy and security terms. We may also use our phone, email and scheduling tools to reply to you.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We only share it when needed to run our service (like the hosting and form tools above) or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you're a client or while we're talking about scheduling, and for a reasonable time after, so we can help if you come back. Ask us any time and we'll delete it.

Your choices and California rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we have about you, and you can opt out of texts at any time. California residents have rights under California privacy law, and we honor them. To make a request, email us. We won't treat you differently for asking.

Children

This website is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top of this page.
