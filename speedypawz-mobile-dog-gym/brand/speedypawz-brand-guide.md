## 2. The story only Speedy PawZ can tell (Uncovery)

Roy Williams' rule: don't tell the whole story, tell your best one. Here is the best one.

### The Sword in the Stone (the one story)
> A Woodland mom with two huskies who needed more running than any schedule allows built a gym on wheels, and now she drives it to your curb so your dog gets the same thing hers do.

Every piece of content should be able to trace back to one of these three beats:
1. **Real dogs, real need.** Kira and Kiko are huskies. Huskies are bred to run. Emelyn knows the "my dog has more energy than my day has hours" problem firsthand.
2. **She built the fix.** A climate-controlled van, two red slat mills, and a promise that the dog sets the pace.
3. **She brings it to you.** Same van that won 2nd place as the Mystery Machine pulls up at your house. Neighbor, not franchise.

### North Star (what success looks like)
Speedy PawZ is **the name Woodland, Davis and Yolo County dog owners think of first and feel best about** when their dog needs more exercise than they can give. Not the cheapest option; the trusted, familiar, local one. [CONFIRM Emelyn's own goal: number of weekly regulars, second van, specific towns]

### What we want to be known for (category dominance)
**"Woodland's mobile dog gym."** She already says it. We own it. Dominate Woodland first, then Davis, then the rest of Yolo and Sacramento.

### The anchor (Pavlov's third key)
Williams says the message must tie to an emotion **already in the customer's heart**. For Speedy PawZ, those emotions are:
- **The quiet guilt** of "my dog needs more than I'm giving them."
- **The relief** of a calm evening: dog asleep on the rug, you on the couch.
- **Pride** in a dog who's confident and happy, especially a shy one.
- **Dog as family.** These owners talk about "my baby," "my boy," "my girl."

Attach Speedy PawZ to the relief and the pride, never to the guilt directly. We name the problem kindly, then paint the calm.

## 5. Who we're talking to

### The dogs (three "who it's for" types, from her own listing)
| Dog type | Owner's problem | What the owner wants to picture |
|---|---|---|
| **The high-energy pup** (huskies, labs, shepherds, young dogs) | Zoomies at 9 pm, chewed shoes, walks don't cut it | A tired, happy dog snoozing after the van pulls away |
| **The older dog slowing down** | Wants them moving gently without overdoing it | Their senior dog strolling at their own speed, tail up |
| **The shy pup** | Dog parks and group daycare are too much | Their dog relaxing one-on-one with Emelyn, treat in sight |

### The owners (personas)
**1. "Busy Brooke," working parent (primary)**
Woodland or Davis, 28–45, kids and a job, loves her dog, feels guilty about short walks. Scrolls IG and FB in the evening. Wants: zero extra effort, someone she trusts, a dog who isn't bouncing off the walls. Fear: paying for something her dog won't use.

**2. "Careful Carl," the worried owner**
Owns a reactive, anxious or senior dog. Avoids parks. Worries about heat, smoke and safety. Wants: private, gentle, supervised. Fear: "Will a treadmill stress my dog out or hurt them?"

**3. "Davis professional," time-poor and convenience-first**
Works long hours or from home, may have a high-drive breed. Wants: book online, someone shows up, photo update hits their phone. Fear: hassle, unclear pricing.

*(Personas are composites for writing purposes, not real clients. Names are placeholders and never appear in client-facing work.)*

### Relational, not transactional
These owners are **relational shoppers** (Williams): their fear is making a poor choice for their dog, not overpaying by $5. They want an expert they can trust. So the brand leads with **Emelyn's face, the process, safety and proof**, and treats price promotions as occasional, honest offers, never the identity.

## 8. Voice and tone

### How Emelyn actually writes (pulled from her posts)
- **Opens with energy.** "WE TOOK 2ND PLACE! 🏆👻" · "🚨 BLACK FRIDAY SPECIAL IS LIVE! 🐾🖤"
- **Short, punchy lines with a wink.** "Same van that brings the dog gym right to your curb, just with a few extra snacks this time. 😉"
- **Gratitude out loud.** "Thank you to everyone who stopped by, voted, and said hi! 🧡"
- **Always grounds it locally.** "📍 Serving Woodland, Davis, Sac & Yolo"
- **Ends with one clear ask.** "Book your pup's first session through the link in bio!"
- **Signs off with the tagline.** "Happy PawZ, Healthy Strides! 🐾"
- **Talks about her own life openly.** "my son's school trunk or treat," "Building this dream together," "You are not behind."
- **Sprinkles playful pop culture.** Mystery Machine, Velma "reporting for duty."

### Voice rules
1. **Write like Emelyn talking to a neighbor over the fence.** First person ("I," "my pups") for personal posts; "we" for business posts. Never "Speedy PawZ LLC is pleased to announce."
2. **Short sentences. Exclamation points welcome** (one or two per post, not every line).
3. **Talk to owners like friends, about dogs like family.** "your pup," "your girl," "your boy," "the pack."
4. **Puns on PawZ are a feature, not a crutch.** One per piece, max. ("Hit the PawZ button on boredom," "PawZ for a nap.")
5. **Racing language as playful accent:** "pit stop," "warm-up lap," "finish line," "checkered flag." Never "crush it," "beast mode," "no pain no gain."
6. **Name the place.** Woodland, Davis, Sacramento, Yolo County. Local beats generic.
7. **One call to action per piece.**
8. **Be specific and true.** Describe what actually happens (slat mill, no motor, praise and treats, photo update). Never inflate.

### Tone by channel
| Channel | Tone dial | Emojis | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IG / FB / TikTok captions** | Full Emelyn: warm, punny, grateful | Yes, sparingly: 🐾 🧡 📍 🏆 (plus 🖤 for Black Friday) | "Kira asked for a warm-up lap and took three. 🐾 📍 Woodland, Davis, Sac & Yolo. Book your pup's first session through the link in bio!" |
| **Reel on-screen text** | Ultra short, one idea per card | No | "Same van that brings the dog gym to your curb" |
| **Meta ad primary text** | Warm but clear; hook, picture, proof, one CTA | Light, 0–2 | "Does your dog still have zoomies after the walk? Speedy PawZ is Woodland's mobile dog gym..." |
| **Ad headlines** | Plain benefit + place | No | "Woodland's Mobile Dog Gym Comes to You" |
| **Landing page** | Friendly, reassuring, clear; still Emelyn | No | "We bring the gym to your curb. Your dog sets the pace." |
| **DMs / texts** | One-to-one, personal, quick | 1 max | "Hi! It's Emelyn from Speedy PawZ 🐾 What's your pup's name?" |
| **Email** | Newsy, personal, short | No (or one in sign-off) | Subject: "Your pup's first lap" |
| **Review requests** | Grateful, easy, no pressure | 1 | "It would mean the world to me..." |

## 10. Messaging pillars

Five pillars. Every piece of content should clearly serve at least one. Rough mix for organic: Proof/Results 25% · Convenience 20% · Safety/Pace 20% · Community 20% · Fun/Behind-the-scenes 15%.

### Pillar 1: We come to you (Convenience)
- **Core message:** Your dog gets a real workout without you driving anywhere.
- **Proof points:** Mobile van, curbside, climate-controlled, serves Woodland/Davis/Sac/Yolo.
- **Sample lines:** "We bring the gym to YOU." · "Your driveway is the new dog park." · "The gym pulls up. Your dog hops in. You finish your coffee."

### Pillar 2: At your dog's pace (Safety and trust)
- **Core message:** No motor, no force, no rush. Your dog sets the speed and Emelyn never leaves their side.
- **Proof points:** Motorless slat mills, praise/toys/treats coaching, continuous monitoring, first-aid kit, low-pressure warm-up.
- **Sample lines:** "No motor. No pushing. Just your pup, at their pace." · "Every dog gets comfortable before they ever take a step."

### Pillar 3: A happier, calmer dog (Results)
- **Core message:** A tired dog is a happy dog. Many owners notice a calmer evening.
- **Proof points:** Google review summary (dogs nap hard after), raffle winners became weekly regulars, post-session photo updates.
- **Sample lines:** "From zoomies to snoozies." · "Thirty minutes on the mill. Three hours on the couch." *(only as playful, never as a guarantee)*
- **Rule:** use "helps burn energy," "many owners notice," "builds confidence." Never "fixes," "cures," "guaranteed."

### Pillar 4: Part of the pack (Community)
- **Core message:** Mom-owned, local, and always showing up for Woodland's people and pups.
- **Proof points:** Yolo County Animal Shelter giveback; PAWS Pet Adoption & Wellness golf tournament raffle (Davis, July 18, 2026); Soul Puppy Woodland Halloween costume contest prize; 2nd place at Adan's school trunk-or-treat.
- **Sample lines:** "Welcome to the pack." · "Thank you, Woodland!" · "Giving back to shelter pups."

### Pillar 5: Fitness should be fun (Personality)
- **Core message:** Racing flags, costumes, trophies, bandanas. Exercise is play.
- **Proof points:** Mystery Machine van, Velma costume, checkered overalls, dogs in bandanas, "MOVE IT" reel.
- **Sample lines:** "Ready, set, zoom." · "Checkered flag for the good girl." · "Warm-up lap complete. 🏁"

## 13. Words to use and words to avoid

### Use
| Use | Instead of |
|---|---|
| mobile dog gym, dog fitness session, dog gym | grooming, spa, daycare |
| slat mill, dog-powered treadmill | treadmill (alone), running machine |
| at your dog's pace, your dog sets the speed | we make your dog run |
| burns off excess energy, helps burn energy | burns X calories, weight loss guaranteed |
| builds confidence, many owners notice | cures anxiety, fixes behavior |
| a happy complement to your daily walks | replaces walks, better than walking |
| one-on-one, private | exclusive, VIP |
| your pup, your dog, the pack | pets, animals, units |
| we come to you, right to your curb | on-site service delivery |
| book your pup's first session | sign up now, buy now |

### Banned (never write)
- Anything that implies **grooming** services.
- **Medical/vet claims or guarantees:** "cures," "treats," "heals," "fixes behavior problems," "guaranteed weight loss," "vet-approved" [unless CONFIRMED in writing].
- Anything implying the dog is **forced, strapped in to run, or left alone.**
- Knocks on owners who walk their dogs ("Walks aren't enough," "lazy owners").
- Invented prices, reviews, testimonials, client names, numbers or credentials.
- Generic AI phrasing: unleash, elevate, game-changer, look no further, in today's busy world, revolutionize, next level.
- "Speedy Paws," "SpeedyPaws," "Speedy PAWZ," "speedy pawz" in body copy. **Always "Speedy PawZ."** (Exception: hashtags and URLs, which stay lowercase: #speedypawz, speedypawz.com.)

### A note on the misspelling
Google auto-corrected "speedypaws woodland" to "speedy paws." People will spell it with an S. Keep "Speedy Paws" in **search-only** places (Google Business Profile description keywords, website meta keywords, ad keyword lists), never in visible brand copy.
