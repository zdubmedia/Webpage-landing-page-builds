FAQ

Straight answers

Is a treadmill safe for my dog?

Our slat mills have no motor, so your dog powers every step. Emelyn stays right beside your dog the whole session, and there's a pet first-aid kit on board. If your dog has a health condition, check with your vet first.

What if my dog won't get on?

That's okay! Not every dog steps right on. Emelyn goes slow with praise, toys and treats and builds confidence through routine. Even when it's not the right fit, it's still an enrichment activity.

Is there water for my dog?

Yes. Fresh water is in the van, and your pup gets breaks whenever they need them.

Why pay when I can walk my dog?

Keep the walks! A session adds a real run at your dog's own speed, rain or shine, without you leaving home.

Where do you go?

Woodland, Davis, West Sacramento, Winters, Sacramento and the rest of Yolo County. Pricing can vary by location. Not sure if you're in range? Send a request and we'll let you know.
