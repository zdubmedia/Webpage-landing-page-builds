Meet your coach

Hi, I'm Emelyn. Welcome to the pack!

Speedy PawZ started with my two huskies, Kira and Kiko. Huskies are born to run, and some days a walk just wasn't enough. Too hot, too smoky, too rainy, or just too busy.

So I created a gym that could go anywhere: a climate-controlled van with two dog-powered slat mills. No motors. No pressure. Just happy dogs moving at their own pace.

Now I bring it right to your door. I'm a mom, a dog lover and a proud part of this community, and I treat every pup like one of my own.

FAQ

Straight answers

Is a treadmill safe for my dog?

Our slat mills have no motor, so your dog powers every step and sets the speed. Emelyn stays with your dog the whole session, coaches with praise, toys and treats, and never forces anything. There's a pet first-aid kit on board. If your dog has a health condition, check with your vet first.

What if my dog won't get on?

That's okay! We keep it low-pressure and let your pup get comfortable first.

Why pay when I can walk my dog?

Keep the walks! Speedy PawZ is a happy complement to them. It's great for high-energy days, busy days, and days when it's too hot, smoky or rainy to be outside.

How long is a session?

About 30 minutes, customized to your dog.

Where do you go?

Woodland, Davis, Sacramento and Yolo County. Not sure if you're in range? Send a request and we'll let you know.

Is this grooming?

Nope! Speedy PawZ is all about fitness and fun. We're a mobile dog gym.
